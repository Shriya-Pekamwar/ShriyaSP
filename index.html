01. About

I'm a Data Engineer with nearly 3 years of experience building and owning production data pipelines. I take messy data from many sources and turn it into clean, trusted, and secure datasets that teams can rely on.

At Mass General Hospital, I integrated 100K+ daily clinical records from 15+ sources, including Epic and REDCap, into curated Snowflake tables. I rebuilt the refresh process around incremental change data capture with Snowpipe, Streams, and Tasks, cutting refresh time by 90%. I also made Snowflake queries about 98% faster and automated manual reporting, reducing prep time by up to 95%. Because the data included patient information, I secured it with role-based access, masking, and row- and column-level security.

At Deloitte, I spent two years building tested data pipelines and APIs, and supported a migration from on-premise SQL Server to Azure without disrupting reporting. I also built a dashboard from metadata across 10+ databases that cut manual audit effort by 60%.

Outside of work, I build end-to-end projects to learn new tools: a healthcare claims pipeline on Databricks with PySpark, dbt, and GitHub Actions; Dockerized Airflow pipelines loading into Snowflake on AWS EC2; a parser that turns financial PDFs into structured data; and a RAG system where a validation layer made LLM outputs 20x more reliable.

I also mentor early-career engineers on resumes, interviews, and breaking into data roles. When I'm away from data, I teach yoga, dance, read, and travel whenever I can.
